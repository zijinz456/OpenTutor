## Top 3 Features to Highlight in Marketing This Week

### 1. Bayesian Knowledge Tracing + Zone of Proximal Development (Show HN draft ready)
This week's community post draft includes a full HN-ready write-up on BKT + ZPD difficulty selection (`knowledge_tracer.py`, 197 lines; `bkt_trainer.py`, 296 lines; `difficulty_selector.py`, 165 lines). This is OpenTutor's most academically rigorous feature: per-concept mastery estimation using the Corbett & Anderson (1994) formulation, upgraded to EM-fitted parameters via pyBKT when ≥15 observations exist. The difficulty selector maps P(L) to a three-layer system with gap-type overrides. **The draft is ready — this HN post should be submitted this week. It's the strongest technical story in the marketing pipeline.**

### 2. Cognitive Load Auto-Tuning (Week 30 build-in-public post live)
The Week 30 post explains the sliding-window weight dampener that prevents saturation in the cognitive load detector: 12 behavioral signals, 20-message rolling window, asymmetric decay/recovery (0.90 decay, 1.04 recovery, 0.40 floor). The engineering decision-making (why not ML, why asymmetric rates) is documented in the post. This is a strong r/LocalLLaMA follow-on story about behavioral signal calibration for local-first AI — **not yet posted to that subreddit from the Week 30 content itself**.

### 3. LECTOR Semantic Spaced Repetition (still zero dedicated marketing)
